# 📊 UDEMY COURSES PERFORMANCE DASHBOARD| EXCEL, POWER BI, DAX, POWER QUERY|

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Udemy course data, focusing on pricing structures, course content metrics, subscriber distribution, and subject performance.

## 📌 Key Metrics Overview
* **Total Courses:** 4K
* **Total Lectures:** ~147K
* **Total Subscribers:** +12M
* **Total Reviews:** 574K
* **Price Range:** Free ($0) to $200 (Average: $66)

---

## 💡 Insights & Features Included
* **Average Price by Subject:** Visual comparison showing top pricing categories (*Web Development*, *Business Finance*, *Graphic Design*, and *Musical Instruments*).
* **Subscriber vs. Duration Analysis:** Correlation between Subscriber and Duration_Time (in minutes).
* **Subscriber Distribution:** Breakdown of subscribers by course pricing category (*Free*, *Cheap*, *Medium*, *Expensive*) and paid status.
* **Content Breakdown:** Ratio of Paid vs. Free lectures and subscribers.
* **Multi-Page Navigation:** Interactive navigation tabs including *Landing Page*, *Overview*, *Price Analysis*, and *Details*.
